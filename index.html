About Me

I got into coding because I wanted to build solutions to problems I saw around me. After seeing how much repetitive manual work people often deal with in daily tasks, I realized so much of it could be solved through tech and began experimenting with code to automate those workflows. Seeing an idea turn into working software that genuinely saved hours of time sparked an obsession with building things from scratch.

Right now, I'm a software engineering student based in Malaysia. Most of my energy goes into building practical web applications and taking them into the real world. Recently, I built Q-Up, a virtual queuing app that eliminates physical line congestion. We tested it live with students and vendors in the university cafeteria, and went on to win a silver medal at the national PITRAM 2026 innovation competition.

I really love the front-end side of development. I have a strong appreciation for clean, minimalist design where every element has a clear purpose and nothing feels cluttered. There is something deeply satisfying about crafting pixel-perfect layouts, stripping away unnecessary visual noise, and designing simple interfaces that just work.
